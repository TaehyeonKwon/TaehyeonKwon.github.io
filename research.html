01 · Planning · Ph.D. research with Dr. Anirudh Subramanyam

Capacity expansion planning under uncertainty

Grid planners decide which generators, transmission lines, and storage to build over decades, before future demand and renewable output are known. Judging a single candidate plan means simulating grid operation over many scenarios and hours, so the full planning problem quickly becomes too large to solve.

02 · Operation · with Dr. Min Kyu Sim’s group, Seoul National University of Science and Technology

Energy storage and microgrid operation

A battery earns money by charging when electricity is cheap and discharging when it is expensive, but every cycle also wears it down. I develop controllers that decide when to charge and discharge while counting that wear as a cost.
